Features

Everything your business runs on, in one app.

Invoices in seconds

Move each invoice from Draft to Issued to Paid. Share it as a PDF over WhatsApp or email, or duplicate last week’s order in one tap.

Draft Issued Paid

Tax done right

VAT is extracted from each line’s gross price and rounded per line: 18/118 on the Mainland, 15/115 in Zanzibar. Your totals match what TRA or ZRA expects, to the shilling.

40 × TSh 11,800 = TSh 472,000 → VAT TSh 72,000 (18%)

Tax period tracking

Each month is marked Open, Review or Filed. Filed periods lock, so nobody can change them later.

Payments

Record full or partial payments by cash, bank or mobile money: M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa and Airtel Money.

Stock tracking

Stock goes down automatically when you issue an invoice, and every movement is recorded.

Clients and products

Save your customers and product catalogue once. Pick them in a tap on every new invoice.

Expenses and insights

Log expenses as they happen. See revenue, expense and tax reports at a glance, and export tax reports as PDF for your accountant, TRA or ZRA.

Works offline, backed up to the cloud

Your data lives on your phone, so NgomeBiz works with no signal. When you’re online it syncs to the cloud and across your devices.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data safe?

Your records are stored on your phone and backed up to the cloud whenever you’re online. If your phone breaks or is stolen, sign in on a new one and everything comes back. Filed tax periods are locked so they can’t be changed by accident.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Create invoices, record payments and log expenses with no connection at all. NgomeBiz syncs automatically the next time you’re online.

Is it TRA and ZRA compliant?

Yes. NgomeBiz supports both VAT systems: 18% for TRA on Tanzania Mainland and 15% for ZRA in Zanzibar. It extracts VAT from gross prices line by line and rounds each line, so your totals match what the tax authority expects. It keeps every monthly period in order. You still submit your return yourself; NgomeBiz makes sure the numbers are ready.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. NgomeBiz syncs across devices, so you and your team can work from the same invoices, stock and customer list on different phones.

Which countries are supported?

NgomeBiz is for businesses in Tanzania Mainland and Zanzibar. Invoicing, payments, stock and expenses work the same on both sides of the channel.
